Frequently Asked Questions

How do I filter games by platform?

Use the platform filter buttons at the top of the dashboard. Click on PC, PS5, Xbox, or Switch to filter. You can select multiple platforms at once. Click "All Platforms" to reset.

What do the score colors mean?

Metacritic scores are color-coded: Green (75-100) means positive reviews, Amber (50-74) means mixed reviews, Red (0-49) means negative reviews, and Gray (TBD) means no score is available yet.

How often is the data updated?

Game data is fetched in real-time from the RAWG Video Games Database API. Data is cached for 5 minutes to provide fast loading. New releases and scores are updated as they become available in the RAWG database.

Can I switch the language?

Yes! Use the language dropdown in the top-right corner of the header. We support English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, German, French, and Portuguese. The interface language is auto-detected from your browser settings.

How do I share a game?

Click on any game card to open its details. In the detail view, you'll find share buttons for Twitter/X, Facebook, and a copy link option. You can also share the entire month's releases using the "Share This Month's Releases" button.

Is this service free?

Yes, NextGen Game Radar is completely free to use. The service is supported by non-intrusive advertisements.
