About NextGen Game Radar

Never Miss a Game Release Again

The video game industry releases thousands of new titles every year across PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo platforms. For passionate gamers, keeping track of what is coming out — and when — has become a genuine challenge. A highly anticipated RPG can land quietly on a busy Tuesday while you were focused on something else. A critically acclaimed indie title can disappear into the noise before you even learn it exists. NextGen Game Radar was built to solve exactly this problem by putting a clear, organized, and real-time view of new and upcoming game releases directly in front of you.

What Is NextGen Game Radar?

NextGen Game Radar is a free, web-based game discovery dashboard that aggregates new and upcoming video game releases in one place. The dashboard pulls data from the RAWG Video Games Database API — the world's largest video game database, with over 800,000 games catalogued — and presents it in a clean, filterable interface that lets you quickly find exactly the games you care about. Whether you want to see everything releasing on PlayStation 5 this month, find upcoming action RPGs with high Metacritic scores, or simply browse what is on the horizon across all platforms, NextGen Game Radar gives you that view instantly, without needing to hop between multiple gaming news sites or storefronts.

The dashboard is updated continuously via the RAWG API, which means release dates, new game additions, and Metacritic score updates flow in automatically as they become available in RAWG's database. You are always looking at current data, not a static snapshot that was refreshed once a week.

Powered by RAWG — The World's Largest Game Database

The foundation of NextGen Game Radar is the RAWG Video Games Database API. RAWG is widely regarded as the most comprehensive video game database available, cataloguing games from the earliest arcade era through the latest current-generation releases. The RAWG database is maintained by a global community of contributors and covers games across dozens of platforms, including PC, PlayStation 1 through 5, all Xbox generations, Nintendo Switch, Wii, Game Boy, iOS, Android, and many more.

For NextGen Game Radar, we specifically leverage RAWG's release date data, platform metadata, genre classifications, and aggregated Metacritic review scores. This rich dataset allows us to give you not just a list of game names, but meaningful context about each release — what platforms it targets, what genre it belongs to, and how critics have received it if reviews are already in. RAWG also provides high-quality game artwork, which makes the dashboard visually engaging and immediately recognizable.

Why Staying Current With Game Releases Matters

For casual players, keeping track of releases is simply about not missing something they would enjoy. But for a growing segment of the gaming community, staying current is a professional necessity.

Game journalists and critics need to know what is releasing so they can prepare reviews, coordinate embargo schedules, and keep their coverage calendars up to date. Missing a major release by even a few days can mean lost traffic and relevance in a highly competitive media landscape.

Content creators on YouTube, Twitch, and other platforms build their content calendars around game releases. A streamer who wants to capture early-release traffic needs to know exactly when a game is launching — and whether it is likely to be worth covering based on early Metacritic scores and genre interest. NextGen Game Radar gives creators the advance visibility they need to plan their schedules strategically.

Gaming enthusiasts and community managers who maintain subreddits, Discord servers, or fan communities around specific franchises or genres need to surface release information to their communities quickly and accurately. Having a reliable, filterable release dashboard means less time hunting for information and more time engaging with your community.

Game developers and industry professionals track release windows to understand the competitive landscape, avoid launching into crowded periods, and monitor how similar titles perform with critics and players.

Key Features

  • Platform filtering — filter game releases by PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch to see only the releases relevant to the hardware you own or cover.
  • Genre filtering — narrow results by genre including Action, RPG, Adventure, Strategy, Sports, Simulation, Puzzle, and more, so you find games that match your interests without scrolling through irrelevant titles.
  • Metacritic score display — each game card shows the Metacritic score with color-coded badges: green for acclaimed titles (75–100), amber for mixed reception (50–74), red for negative reviews (0–49), and gray for games not yet scored.
  • Upcoming and recent releases organized by month — releases are grouped chronologically so you can see what is launching this month, next month, and further ahead.
  • Sort options — sort games by release date, Metacritic score, or alphabetically by name to find what you need quickly.
  • 8-language support — the interface is fully localized in English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese, with automatic detection of your browser's language preference.
  • Responsive design — the dashboard works cleanly on desktop browsers, tablets, and mobile phones, so you can check upcoming releases from any device.
  • No account required — access the full dashboard immediately with no sign-up, no email address, and no subscription.

Who Uses NextGen Game Radar?

Our users span the full spectrum of gaming culture. Hardcore enthusiasts who follow every major release use the dashboard as their go-to release calendar. Casual players check in monthly to see if anything interesting has launched that they might have missed. Streamers and YouTubers bookmark the dashboard as a content planning tool. Game journalists reference it when building their editorial calendars. No matter where you fall on the gaming spectrum, if you care about what is new in video games, NextGen Game Radar gives you a faster and more organized way to stay informed.
