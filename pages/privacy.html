Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Overview

NextGen Game Radar ("we," "our," or "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit our website at nextgen-game-radar.vercel.app, how that information is used, and what choices you have. Please read this document carefully. By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have reviewed and understood these practices. If you have any questions, our contact information is provided at the end of this policy.

NextGen Game Radar is a free, browser-based game discovery dashboard. We have designed it with a minimal data footprint. We do not require user registration, do not maintain user accounts, and do not store personal game preference data on any server we control.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically by Hosting Infrastructure

When you visit the Service, standard HTTP server logs may be generated by Vercel, our hosting provider. These logs may include your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, and the date and time of each page request. This logging is standard hosting behavior and is not unique to our Service. We do not access or use these logs to build user profiles. Please review Vercel's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle hosting data.

2.2 Information Collected via the RAWG API

NextGen Game Radar retrieves game data from the RAWG Video Games Database API (api.rawg.io). When the dashboard fetches game data, your browser makes requests to the RAWG API server. These requests may include your IP address and browser information as part of standard HTTP headers. RAWG's handling of this data is governed by RAWG's Privacy Policy. We do not transmit any personally identifiable information about you to RAWG. We do not store the results of RAWG API calls on any server we control; data is displayed in your browser and cached locally for performance.

2.3 Data Stored Locally in Your Browser

The Service stores a small amount of data in your browser's localStorage:

  • Language preference: The display language you have selected (e.g., "en" for English, "ja" for Japanese). Storing this locally prevents you from needing to reselect your language on each visit.
  • Visitor counter: A non-identifying local numeric counter that increments with each visit. This data never leaves your device.
  • API response cache: Recently fetched game data from RAWG is cached locally for approximately five minutes to improve performance and reduce API calls. This cached data is the same public game information displayed on screen and contains no personal data.

All localStorage data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time through your browser's site data settings.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

The Service itself does not set first-party tracking cookies. However, we serve third-party advertisements to support the free operation of the Service. These advertising partners use cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar technologies to identify your browser across websites and to deliver interest-based advertising. Details about each advertising partner are provided below.

We participate in Google AdSense, operated by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA). Google AdSense serves advertisements on this Service and uses cookies and similar technologies to personalize ads based on your browsing history and inferred interests across websites that use Google advertising products. Google may also use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your visit to our Service and other sites on the internet. You can view Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy. To opt out of personalized advertising from Google, visit Google Ads Settings or use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out tool.

3.2 Adsterra

We may serve advertisements from Adsterra Network. Adsterra is a digital advertising platform that may use cookies and device identifiers to deliver targeted advertisements and to measure advertising effectiveness. Adsterra does not directly collect personally identifiable information from end users, but its cookies may be used to build an anonymous interest profile linked to your browser. For more information, review Adsterra's Privacy Policy. You may be able to opt out of interest-based advertising through Your Online Choices or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information associated with your visit is used solely to:

  • Deliver and display the game release dashboard and its content;
  • Remember your language preference between visits;
  • Serve advertisements through our advertising partners as described above;
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues with the Service.

We do not use any information to build personal profiles, engage in behavioral tracking beyond what our third-party ad partners do within their own systems, or sell data to any third party.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you the following rights with respect to personal data:

  • Right of access: Request confirmation of whether we process personal data about you and receive a copy.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data we hold.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing or based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request limitation on how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

Because we do not directly collect personal data beyond what advertising partners may collect within their own systems, most of these rights apply primarily to your interaction with Google and Adsterra. To exercise rights related to those partners' data, contact them directly. To exercise rights in connection with any communication data you have sent to us, contact taeshinkim11@gmail.com.

6. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Users)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) grants you the right to know what personal information is collected about you, the right to request deletion of that information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. Our third-party advertising partners may collect certain data as described in Section 3. To opt out of that data collection for advertising purposes, follow the opt-out links provided in Section 3. For CCPA-related requests or questions, contact us at taeshinkim11@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

All data transmission between your browser and the Service, including API requests to RAWG, is protected by HTTPS encryption. Because we do not operate a server-side database of user information, the risk of a data breach affecting your personal information through our Service directly is minimal. However, no internet communication is entirely immune to interception, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of data processed by third-party advertising or hosting providers. We encourage you to review their security practices in their respective policies.

8. Children's Privacy

While video games as a category include titles appropriate for all ages, our Service is intended for use by adults and older teenagers capable of understanding these terms. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email address below so we can take appropriate action.
